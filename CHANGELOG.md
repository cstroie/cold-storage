# Changelog

Releases carry `COLDSTOR.COM` and `COLDSTOR.DAT`; copy both onto a disk, side by side.

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-13)

- The title screen's "press a key" is centred under the name.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

First release.

- You are WREN, the last mind awake on a colony ship: wake one of forty sleepers at a time, talk to
  them, and what you ask is what they dream. Waking costs coolant; the ship has events, years go
  by, and the game saves and resumes. No hurry, no game over.
- Z80, CP/M 2.2, 80x24, VT100 / VT52 / ADM-3A, plain ASCII. The game's words are in
  `coldstor.txt`, packed into `COLDSTOR.DAT` by `mkdat.js`.
